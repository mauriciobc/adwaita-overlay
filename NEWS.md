# adwaita-overlay NEWS

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- libadwaita/GTK4 overlay sheet, installed as a theme directory and, per
  user, through `adwaita-overlay-install` (with `--update` and `--uninstall`).
- Selector-contract checker (`check-selectors`) and an optional pacman hook
  (`-Dpacman_hook=true`).
- The GTK3 front is not included.
